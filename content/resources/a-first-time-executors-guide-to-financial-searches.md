Being named an executor for the first time can be daunting, particularly when it comes to identifying every financial account the deceased held. This guide covers the basics.

## Where to start

- Gather any paperwork, statements, or correspondence found in the deceased's home
- Check for standing orders or direct debits on any accounts you can already access
- Consider whether a formal financial asset search is appropriate for the size and complexity of the estate

## Authority to act

Before an institution will discuss an account with you, they will usually expect to see either a grant of probate, letters of administration, or evidence that you are named as executor in the will.
